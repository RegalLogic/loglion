# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 5, 2025**

## 1. Data Collection

Log Lion does not collect any personal data directly from users through the application. However, the application may access certain types of data stored on your device, such as log files, to provide its features.

**Purchases via Microsoft Store:** When you purchase or download Log Lion from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect certain data regarding your purchase and usage of the application. This data collection is governed by Microsoft's privacy policies.

**Purchases outside of Microsoft Store:** When a user or business purchases a license for Log Lion outside of the Microsoft Store, we collect basic contact information including first and last names, organization name, and email addresses. This information is collected to manage licenses and provide customer support.

## 2. Data Usage

The data accessed by Log Lion is used solely within the application for the purpose of enhancing your user experience and enabling specific functionalities, such as:

- Analyzing device performance through log files.
- Troubleshooting and diagnosing issues.

The contact information collected from purchases outside of the Microsoft Store is used for:

- Managing and verifying licenses.
- Displaying the license owner's information within the application.
- Providing customer support.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Log Lion does not use any third-party services or SDKs that collect or process user data. However, data related to your purchase and use of the application may be collected by Microsoft as part of their app store metrics.

## 4. User Rights

Since Log Lion does not collect or store personal data through the application itself, there are no specific user rights concerning personal data collection and storage by Log Lion. If you have questions about the data collected by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store, please refer to Microsoft's privacy policy.

If you have provided contact information for license management, you can contact us to review, update, or delete your information by emailing contact@regallogic.com.

## 5. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will notify users by updating the date at the top of this policy and, if significant changes are made, by providing a prominent notice within the application.

## Additional Information

For more information about the data collection and privacy practices of the Microsoft Store, please refer to the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

The current policy is published at **https://www.regallogic.com/PrivacyPolicy.htm**
