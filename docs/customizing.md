# Customizing

Log Lion remembers how you leave it. Window size and position, which panes are open and where, column widths and order, toolbar layout, your filters and your recent files all persist between sessions without you doing anything.

This section covers the parts you change deliberately.

## Starting again

If the window ends up in a state you cannot undo — a pane lost off-screen, a toolbar emptied by accident — nothing is permanent. **Tools ▸ Customize** restores the toolbars and menus, panes come back from **View ▸ Tool Bars & Panes**, and the individual settings files described in [Where Log Lion Keeps Your Data](data-folder.md) can be deleted to return that feature to its defaults. Deleting one of those files never affects your logs.
